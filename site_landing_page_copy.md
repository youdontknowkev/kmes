**Frequently Asked Questions**

* **How does your pricing work?**
We keep our rates simple and transparent: **$100/hour plus parts**, with a standard **$100 bench diagnostic fee**. That bench fee is the maximum you’ll ever pay just for diagnostics, covering the thorough physical and electronic inspection required to pinpoint the exact issue.
* **Why is there a bench fee?**
Opening up vintage and hand-wired analog equipment safely takes time and careful handling. Simply disassembling a multi-board chassis, isolating the circuit on an oscilloscope, tracing intermittent faults, and safely reassembling the unit is skilled bench labor in itself. The diagnostic fee ensures your gear gets a thorough, unhurried evaluation without taking shortcuts.
* **Will I be surprised by a large bill?**
Never. Once we complete our initial assessment, **we call you directly** to discuss our findings, provide an honest appraisal of the gear's overall health, and give you an exact estimate. We never perform billable repairs without your explicit approval first.
* **Can you fix my modern digital synth, micro-component drum machine, or DSP pedal?**
Generally, no. Our bench is specifically equipped for through-hole analog circuits, point-to-point wiring, and serviceable discrete components. Modern surface-mount (SMD) and micro-digital gear often rely on multi-layer proprietary boards designed for board replacement rather than component-level repair. While modern digital gear is great, we stick to our core strength: keeping serviceable analog and classic tube designs running for decades to come.
* **Do you take on special projects like amp kit builds, DIY troubleshooting, or Eurorack gear?**
Yes, depending on current bench capacity. Whether you started a 5F1 Tweed kit build that needs troubleshooting and biasing, want a custom hand-wired circuit built, or have an analog Eurorack module acting up, reach out and let’s discuss the project.
